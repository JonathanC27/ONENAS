\section*{Prediction rule: single champion vs.\ island-champion ensemble}

Both rules are extracted from the \emph{same} completed runs -- identical
evolved population, seed, panel, and market days -- so each paired
difference isolates the prediction rule alone. Booked identically
(sleeves top-10/H=10, netted TC/PRC costs); cells are the mean over four
panels within seed, then over seeds; $\Delta$ = ensemble $-$ single, paired
$t$ over seeds.

\begin{tablenotes}\footnotesize
\item[a] The published ONE-NAS prediction rule (single global-best genome).
Note the winner's curse: widening 8$\to$40 islands \emph{degrades} the
single champion ($+8.3 \to +4.5$ on 2022--24) while the ensemble improves
($+20.5 \to +27.5$) -- more islands are more lottery tickets for a fitness
signal that cannot rank true quality, and more diversity for an average
that can harvest it. Daily rank-IC deltas tell the same story
($+0.0096$, $t{=}9.8$ at 40 islands).
\item[b] Robustness replication on the 2016--2019 tuning-span fleets, a
span that played no role in the decision to adopt the ensemble rule. All
economic deltas replicate ($t \ge 4.5$); the sole non-significant cell is
the 8-island IC delta ($+0.0011$, $t{=}0.95$).
\end{tablenotes}
